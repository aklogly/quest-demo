Rachel Costin

Ed.S, LPC, NCC

Licensed Professional Counselor

Telehealth

Rachel uses play therapy for children, and sand tray and client-centered therapy across the lifespan. She believes that we all have the ability and desire to continue to adapt and grow, while sometimes needing a hand in getting there. She uses an attachment-based perspective that looks at how our early relationships impact our view of ourselves and our world. She has experience using CBT to help adjust our thoughts and explore how our thoughts about the world frame our experiences.

Get to Know Rachel

Why did you become a mental health professional?

I began advocating for the importance of mental health from my teenage years. After receiving my bachelor’s degree, I spent a few years working in childcare on a military base. Seeing the variety of moves, life events and stressors experienced by military children inspired me to focus on work with children in adjusting to changes that don’t always make sense to them.

What are your interests outside of work?

Outside of work, I enjoy crafting, gaming, reading, spending time with friends and family, and traveling.
