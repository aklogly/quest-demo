Ann M. Sterle

MS, LPCS, LMHC, NCC, MAC, CDCS

Owner Licensed Professional Counselor Supervisor

Fairbanks

Ann M. Sterle is a Licensed Professional Counselor Supervisor in Alaska, a Licensed Mental Health Counselor in Washington and a certified Chemical Dependency Counselor Supervisor. She has worked with adults and adolescents experiencing a variety of mental health and substance use issues.

She is experienced in trauma-focused approaches and certified in Eye Movement Desensitization and Reprocessing (EMDR). She has experience in Cognitive Behavioral Therapy, Dialectical Behavioral Therapy, Mindfulness and Somatic Experiencing. She uses a collaborative approach, supporting clients throughout their healing journey.

Get to Know Ann

Why did you become a mental health professional?

I started my mental health career out of a desire to help people heal. I believe people have the ability to heal when given the right resources and support. During my work in the field of substance abuse counseling, I noticed how substance abuse and mental health were deeply connected. I decided to pursue a Masters in Clinical Mental Health Counseling in order to provide a wider range of therapeutic interventions to better assist my clients in their healing journey.

What are your interests outside of work?

I enjoy spending time with my family and friends, fishing, hiking, traveling, photography and being out in nature.
