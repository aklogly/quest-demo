Riley Koldenhoven

EdD, LCSW

Licensed Clinical Social Worker

Fairbanks

I specialize in working with adults and late adolescents who have experienced various forms of abuse, trauma, identity and relationship issues. Some of my areas of expertise include crisis intervention, interpersonal violence and domestic violence, military families and individuals, and LGBTQIA+ communities.

I use an integrative lens guided by Trauma Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (T-CBT), Dialectical Behavior Therapy (DBT), Internal Family Systems (IFS), neurotherapy, and somatic work.

My therapeutic approach is guided by compassion-based modalities, a person-centered approach, and an emphasis on trauma-informed care. I work with clients to promote self-curiosity, identity alignment, boundary-setting, and improve their interpersonal dynamics. My style is collaborative, strengths-based, and considerate of the social and cultural context of clients’ experiences.

Get to Know Riley

Why did you become a mental health professional?

I am honored to work with people who choose to prioritize their wellness and invest in their healing. Ultimately, I am inspired by the clients I work with and take great joy in witnessing people reclaim their lives and embrace their authenticity.

What are your interests outside of work?

I find peace in the outdoors and spend my weekends packrafting, paragliding, and soaking in fresh air and sunshine. I love cooking and baking, hot yoga, and spending time with my partner and our dog.
