Amanda Speer

LPC, LMHC, CPC, CDC-I

Licensed Professional Counselor

Anchorage · Telehealth

My primary focus is to help clients discover the best version of themselves in a safe, non-judgmental environment.

As an Air Force Veteran, I have an extensive knowledge of military and veteran concerns. My approach to therapy is eclectic to custom fit the needs of any client. With a trauma informed lens, I seek to make therapy available to diverse and underserved populations such as the LGBTQIA+ and neurodivergent people.

I am here to establish a trusting and helping relationship with each client. Mental health is the balanced development of the individual’s personality and emotional attitudes which enable them to live harmoniously in their community. My goal is to establish a trusting relationship with each client to ensure they feel seen, heard and understood.

Get to Know Amanda

My mother is a recovering addict and alcoholic; she has now been clean and sober for the last decade. I became a therapist because I wanted to understand why people do the things they do. I always wanted to learn how to be the best version of myself possible.

What are your interests outside of work?

Outside of work I enjoy nature activities such as hiking, 4 wheeling, camping, fishing and traveling. Some of my favorite indoor activities include art, music and theater.
