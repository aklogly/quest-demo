Carly Lyle

LMSW

Licensed Master Social Worker

Fairbanks

I offer counseling in the shape of companionship, sanctuary, and a safe harbor in the storms of life. My aim is, first and foremost, connection. A trusting relationship and emotional safety will enable us to explore together the hurting, confusing, and intimidating places we all experience.

The decision to begin counseling is not an easy one. Facing yourself, your trauma, and your pain takes real courage. My mission and passion are to guide you sensitively and surely towards finding and embracing your courage, your clarity, and your power.

I have worked with individuals, groups, and in crisis intervention, supporting a wide variety of people with different backgrounds, ages, genders, and nationalities. We all have the same needs in common: connection, healing, and peace.

I approach mental health with the belief that individuals are the experts of their own lives; we know ourselves best. So, I use a collaborative approach in supporting the individual throughout their healing journey. I strive to work with individuals to uncover areas that hold them back from healing, help identify long term problems and solutions, explore the roots of their pain and negative behavioral patterns, and engage in relational work that is individualized for each person. No two people are alike; therefore, no two therapy approaches should be either.

Get to Know Carly

Why did you become a mental health professional?

I spent my early adulthood traveling the world, from Cuba to Cambodia, Ecuador to the United Kingdom, and saw pain and despair everywhere. I worked different avenues: supporting women in red light districts, children living in garbage dumps, and so many other awful situations, trying to help ease people’s pain in any way I could. Eventually, I returned to school to pursue an education in social work. I realized I could not save the world. I couldn’t even save one person, but I could provide a safe and nurturing environment for hurting individuals to save themselves.

What are your interests outside of work?

When my child or husband isn’t after my full attention, I love a hot cup of coffee and an engrossing book. I enjoy making beaded earrings, researching psychedelic medicine, astrology, and fun times with friends. I love a good, hard laugh.
