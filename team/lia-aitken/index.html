Lia Aitken

MA, LPCS, NCC

Owner Licensed Professional Counselor Supervisor

Lia Aitken is a Licensed Professional Counselor Supervisor committed to working with adolescents and adults, with experience in individual therapy, group therapy and crisis intervention. That experience spans a variety of mental health settings, including outpatient community behavioral health, residential treatment and private practice.

Lia uses a collaborative approach incorporating EMDR, Brain Mapping and Brain Integration Therapy, Ketamine Assisted Psychotherapy (KAP) and other trauma modalities to help individuals identify long-term issues, uncover barriers to healing and explore the origin of behavioral patterns, with a holistic approach to encourage wellness in every area of life.

Get to Know Lia

Why did you become a mental health professional?

I lived overseas for 20 years and spent 10 of those years in Jordan working with Iraqi and Syrian refugees. I saw the traumatic impact of war and decided to pursue a degree in counseling with the desire to specialize in trauma counseling, to help people process past trauma and work to overcome the impact of these experiences.

What are your interests outside of work?

I enjoy hiking, biking, gardening, camping and being outdoors. I also enjoy traveling and spending time with family and friends.
