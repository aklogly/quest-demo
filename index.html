Your Journey to Wellness

Compassionate counseling and wellness support.

Counseling for children, teens and adults in Fairbanks, in Anchorage and by telehealth across Alaska.

Request an Appointment

How to Get Started

  1. Request an Appointment

    Send a request or call 907-891-7877 and tell us whether you prefer Fairbanks, Anchorage or telehealth. You do not need to share anything about your health yet.

  2. Complete Intake

    Before your first visit, you can share intake information through our secure client portal. Please do not send health details by email or through this website.

  3. Begin Care

    Meet with your clinician in person at our Fairbanks or Anchorage office, or by telehealth from anywhere in Alaska.

We accept most insurance plans. Call 907-891-7877 to ask about your coverage before your first visit.
