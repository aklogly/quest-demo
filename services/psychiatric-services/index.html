How Appointments Work

Psychiatric services are provided by Jef Bayless of Sagebrush Psychiatry. Appointments are mainly by telehealth, so you can access care from the comfort of your home. Jef is also available for in-person appointments at Quest at designated times throughout the year.

Why Quest Partners With Sagebrush

We understand that living in Alaska can sometimes make it challenging to access consistent, high-quality psychiatric care. That is why we have partnered with Jef Bayless of Sagebrush Psychiatry to help bridge that gap. Together, we are making expert mental health support more accessible for our community.

If you are not sure whether to start with psychiatric care or counseling, call Quest at 907-891-7877.
