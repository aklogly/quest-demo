What It Is

Ketamine Assisted Psychotherapy, or KAP, is psychotherapy that works alongside IV ketamine therapy. Quest offers KAP in collaboration with Ketwell Alaska.

How It Works at Quest

KAP is an integrated approach to enhance the effects of IV ketamine therapy and assist you through the experience of working through your feelings and thought processes. Your clinician can serve as a safety anchor while assisting you in your work towards the incorporation of progress achieved.

Your work with Quest starts with an integrated assessment, which must be completed before integration sessions. After that, you meet with your clinician for individual integration therapy sessions.

Quest clinicians who provide KAP are certified to provide psychotherapy with ketamine infusion therapy.

Who It May Help

Ketamine therapy is used for conditions including migraines, treatment-resistant depression, anxiety, neuropathic pain, obsessive compulsive disorder (OCD), post-traumatic stress disorder (PTSD) and fibromyalgia.

Results vary from person to person.

How to Start
