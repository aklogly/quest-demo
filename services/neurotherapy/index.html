What It Is

Neurotherapy measures and trains the electrical signals your brain produces. It uses electrodes placed on the head and a stimulation protocol set for you. The electrodes are non-invasive and do not penetrate the skin. No medication is involved.

How It Works at Quest

Brain Mapping

The first step in neurotherapy is getting a brain map, also called a QEEG. It measures your brainwaves and shows how your brain is functioning. A clinician uses the results to set a neurostimulation protocol for you.

Following Your Map to Wellness

  • Mile Marker 1. Your personalized neurostimulation protocol aims to change the way your brain responds to certain stimuli and assist you in regaining voluntary control over your responses.
  • Mile Marker 2. Your protocol is implemented over 15 sessions, paced at 3 sessions per week. Sessions take place in a private room with one of our clinicians. Neurostimulation lasts forty minutes, and with setting up and cleaning up, each session lasts one hour.
  • Mile Marker 3. We remap and compare results to assess progress and review your treatment plan.

Quest offers neurotherapy in Fairbanks.

Who It May Help

People come to Quest for neurotherapy with concerns such as ADD/ADHD, addiction, anxiety, depression, sleep disorders, PTSD, chronic pain, panic attacks, OCD, brain fog, and trouble with memory, concentration or focus. Some come for peak performance.

Results vary from person to person.
